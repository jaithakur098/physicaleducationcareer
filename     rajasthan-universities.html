Why Choose Rajasthan for Physical Education?

Rajasthan has become one of India's fastest growing destinations for Physical Education courses. Every year thousands of students take admission in D.P.Ed., B.P.Ed., B.P.E.S., M.P.Ed. and Ph.D. programmes across Government and Private Universities.

Many universities are recognised by UGC, approved by NCTE, accredited by NAAC and follow NEP 2020 guidelines. Students also get sports infrastructure, experienced faculty, research opportunities and placement support.

Recognition & Approvals

Before taking admission in any Physical Education programme, every student should verify the university and college approvals.

  • ✅ University Grants Commission (UGC)
  • ✅ National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE)
  • ✅ NAAC Accreditation
  • ✅ AIU Membership (where applicable)
  • ✅ State Government Recognition
  • ✅ Affiliation Status
  • ✅ Valid B.P.Ed. / M.P.Ed. Recognition Order
