B.P.Ed II semester papers

These downloads are practice papers hosted on this site. They are not the official university question paper for your enrolment year. Use them to time yourself and to see how theory papers mix definitions with application.

Paper codes (EC-201, CC-202) follow a common NCTE-era B.P.Ed structure. Your university may use different codes. Match the topic, not only the code.

EC-201 — Contemporary issues in Physical Education, fitness and wellness

Typical themes: wellness vs fitness, lifestyle diseases, doping awareness at a teacher-education level, and current issues in school PE. After the paper, revise Class 11 fitness and wellness and Class 12 nutrition if the questions overlap school PE language.

Download EC-201 paper (PDF)

CC-202 — Educational technology and methods of teaching in PE

Expect teaching methods (command, practice, reciprocal), lesson parts, and simple educational technology (charts, video, ICT in a PE class). Pair with lesson planning.

Download CC-202 paper (PDF) — filename on the server is test.pdf; open it and confirm the heading matches CC-202 before you treat it as that paper.
