About this test

This online test covers Class 12 Unit 2 — Children and Women in Sports. Questions follow the CBSE pattern, mixing one-mark recall items with short conceptual questions, so the test checks whether you can reproduce the syllabus points and not merely recognise them.

Syllabus covered

  • Motor development and factors affecting it
  • Exercise guidelines at different stages of growth and development
  • Common postural deformities: kyphosis, lordosis, scoliosis, flat foot, knock knees, bow legs
  • Corrective measures for each deformity
  • Women's participation in sports in India: sociological and psychological aspects
  • Female athlete triad: osteoporosis, amenorrhoea and eating disorders

How to attempt it

  • Revise the unit notes once, then attempt the test without looking at them.
  • Attempt every question; there is no negative marking, and a considered guess is better than a blank.
  • Note down every question you got wrong and rewrite the correct answer in one line in your revision notebook.
  • Re-attempt the same test after two days. Recall improves sharply on the second spaced attempt.
  • Once you score consistently well, move to a full previous year paper against a three-hour clock.
