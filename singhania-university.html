About Singhania University

Singhania University is a UGC-recognised private university located at Pacheri Bari, Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan. The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across multiple disciplines. The School of Physical Education, Sports & Yoga Sciences provides professional education with practical sports training and academic learning for students interested in careers in teaching, coaching and sports management.
