Singhania University, Pacheri Bari (Jhunjhunu)

Singhania University is a private university whose campus is commonly described as Pacheri Bari, Jhunjhunu district — not the same legal entity as JJT University, even though both are in the same district. Mixing application forms and fee receipts is a common error.

Official site listed in earlier notes: singhaniauniversity.co.in. Confirm PE programmes and NCTE status there. No fees or rankings on this page.

How it differs from JJT in the same district

  • Different website, different accounts office, different NCTE file if any.
  • Different hostel and bus routes — visit both if you are comparing Shekhawati options.
  • JJT has a public PE department page describing a 400 m track; Singhania’s current PE facilities must be checked on its site and on a visit, not copied from JJT’s description.

Admission checks

  1. Programme name on the prospectus matches the name you will later show at PTI document verification.
  2. PFT list published for this session.
  3. Written fee structure before any cash or UPI to a personal number.
