About Madhav University

Madhav University is located near Abu Road in Sirohi district, Rajasthan. The university offers a dedicated Department of Physical Education under the Faculty of Education & Physical Education. The department provides D.P.Ed., B.P.Ed., M.P.Ed. and Ph.D. programmes with a focus on sports science, fitness, coaching, teaching and research. The university highlights modern infrastructure, practical training, skill-based learning and technology integration for students. D.P.Ed. Programme

The Diploma in Physical Education (D.P.Ed.) is designed for students who want to build a career in school physical education, coaching and sports instruction.
