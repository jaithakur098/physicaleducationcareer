Fundamentals of Anatomy, Physiology and Kinesiology in Sports

Anatomy, physiology and kinesiology are important branches of sports science. They help us understand the structure, functions and movement of the human body.

Anatomy

Anatomy is the study of the structure of the human body and its parts.

Physiology

Physiology is the study of how different organs and systems of the body function.

Kinesiology

Kinesiology is the study of human movement and physical activity.

Importance in Sports

  • Improves sports performance
  • Prevents injuries
  • Enhances fitness
  • Helps in scientific training
