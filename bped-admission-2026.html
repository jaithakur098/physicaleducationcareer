Bachelor of Physical Education Complete Guide

What is B.P.Ed?

B.P.Ed (Bachelor of Physical Education) is a professional degree course for students who want to build a career in Physical Education, Sports Coaching, Fitness Training and Sports Management.

Course Name: Bachelor of Physical Education (B.P.Ed)
Duration: 2 Years
Eligibility: Graduation from recognized university
Admission: Merit / Entrance Test / Physical Test

B.P.Ed Eligibility 2026

Students must have completed Graduation from a recognized university. Many universities also conduct physical fitness tests and skill tests during admission.

  • Graduation Pass
  • Minimum 50% Marks (University wise)
  • Physical Fitness Test
  • Sports Participation Preferred

Admission Process

The admission process generally includes:

  1. Online Registration
  2. Document Verification
  3. Physical Fitness Test
  4. Sports Skill Test
  5. Merit List
  6. Final Admission

Career Opportunities After B.P.Ed

  • Physical Education Teacher (PET)
  • Sports Coach
  • Fitness Trainer
  • Sports Officer
  • Gym Instructor
  • Sports Manager
  • School Sports Coordinator

Higher Education Options

After completing B.P.Ed students can pursue:

  • M.P.Ed
  • NIS Coaching Course
  • Ph.D in Physical Education
  • Sports Psychology
  • Sports Management

Why Choose Physical Education Career?

Physical Education provides excellent opportunities in teaching, coaching, fitness industry and government jobs. With increasing awareness of sports and fitness, demand for qualified professionals is growing every year.
