What is B.P.Ed?

B.P.Ed (Bachelor of Physical Education) is a professional degree course for students who want to build a career in Physical Education, Sports Coaching, Fitness Training and Sports Management.

Course Name: Bachelor of Physical Education (B.P.Ed)
Duration: 2 Years
Eligibility: Graduation from recognized university
Admission: Merit / Entrance Test / Physical Test

B.P.Ed Eligibility 2026

Students must have completed Graduation from a recognized university. Many universities also conduct physical fitness tests and skill tests during admission.

B.P.Ed at a glance

The Bachelor of Physical Education is a two-year professional degree, spread over four semesters, and it is the standard qualification for physical education teaching at secondary school level in India. Because it is a teacher education programme, it is regulated by the National Council for Teacher Education, and recognition of the specific programme at the specific institution is what makes the degree usable in government recruitment.

Eligibility

  • A bachelor's degree in any discipline, normally with a minimum of 50 per cent marks; relaxation is generally available for reserved categories as per university and NCTE norms.
  • Candidates with participation in sports at inter-college, inter-university, district, state or national level are usually given weightage, and some universities relax the marks requirement for such candidates.
  • Candidates who have completed a bachelor's degree with Physical Education as a subject, or who hold a D.P.Ed, are eligible under the conditions specified by the university.
  • A medical fitness certificate is required, since the programme involves substantial physical activity.

Selection process

  • Application: apply through the university's admission portal within the notified window; keep scanned documents ready in the prescribed size and format.
  • Written or entrance test: usually covers general physical education knowledge, general awareness, and aptitude for teaching.
  • Physical efficiency test: commonly includes a sprint, an endurance run, a jump event, a throw event and body flexibility or strength items.
  • Sports certificate weightage: marks are added for the highest level of participation, subject to verification of original certificates.
  • Merit list and counselling: the final list combines the written test, the physical test, academic marks and sports weightage in the ratio each university specifies.
  • Document verification and fee payment: originals are checked at this stage; an unrecognised qualifying degree is rejected here.

Course structure

The four semesters combine theory and practical work. Theory papers typically include the history and foundations of physical education, anatomy and physiology, health education, sports psychology and sociology, educational technology and methods of teaching, organisation and administration, sports management, kinesiology and biomechanics, sports medicine, and research and statistics. Practical work covers track and field, gymnastics, yoga, swimming where available, major and minor games, teaching practice lessons, and an internship in a school.

How to prepare

  • Train for the physical test from today. Endurance and technique take months, not weeks. Work on the 100 metre sprint, an 800 or 1500 metre run, shot put, long jump and general strength.
  • Revise physical education theory. The written test rewards clear knowledge of fitness components, anatomy basics, rules of major games and current sports affairs.
  • Follow sports current affairs. Recent Olympic, Asian Games, Commonwealth Games and national championship results are commonly asked.
  • Organise your certificates now. Sports weightage is only granted against original, properly attested certificates.
  • Shortlist recognised institutions early. Check NCTE recognition and intake before the application window closes.

After B.P.Ed

The most common route is school teaching as a PET or PTI, through state recruitment boards, KVS, NVS, Sainik Schools, Army Public Schools or private school recruitment. Other routes include coaching after an NIS diploma, fitness and strength and conditioning work, sports management and administration, and postgraduate study through M.P.Ed followed by a Ph.D for college teaching.
