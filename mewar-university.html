About Mewar University

Mewar University is a multidisciplinary private university located in Chittorgarh, Rajasthan. The university offers professional education in Physical Education through its Faculty of Physical Education. Students receive academic instruction together with practical sports training, coaching experience and research opportunities.

The university aims to prepare Physical Education teachers, coaches, fitness professionals and researchers by combining classroom teaching with practical sports activities and modern educational practices.

Faculty of Physical Education

The Faculty of Physical Education at Mewar University focuses on preparing competent Physical Education teachers, coaches, fitness professionals and researchers. The curriculum combines theoretical knowledge with practical training, sports coaching, health education and sports science. Students participate in practical sessions, seminars, workshops, internships and research activities to develop professional skills.

D.P.Ed. Programme

The Diploma in Physical Education (D.P.Ed.) programme is intended for students interested in teaching physical education at the elementary level, coaching and sports instruction. The programme includes physical fitness, games, teaching methods and sports skill development.

Eligibility

  • 10+2 or equivalent qualification.
  • Admission according to university rules.
  • Sports participation may be considered where applicable.

B.P.Ed. Programme

The Bachelor of Physical Education (B.P.Ed.) programme is a professional teacher education course designed for graduates who wish to become Physical Education Teachers, PETs, coaches and sports professionals. The programme includes sports psychology, biomechanics, exercise physiology, pedagogy, sports management and teaching practice.

Eligibility

  • Graduation from a recognised university.
  • Admission according to the latest university notification.
  • Document verification and eligibility fulfilment are required.

M.P.Ed. Programme

The Master of Physical Education (M.P.Ed.) programme provides advanced education in sports science, coaching methodology, exercise physiology, biomechanics, sports psychology and research methodology. It is designed for candidates interested in higher education, research and professional coaching.

Eligibility

  • B.P.Ed. degree from a recognised institution.
  • Admission according to the current university admission rules.
  • Applicants should verify the latest prospectus for detailed eligibility.

Ph.D. in Physical Education

Mewar University offers doctoral research opportunities in Physical Education for eligible candidates. Research areas may include sports science, physical education, coaching, health education, exercise science and related disciplines. Admission is governed by the university's Ph.D. regulations.
