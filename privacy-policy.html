Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Physical Education Career ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit physicaleducationcareer.in (the "Website"). By using the Website you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: name, email address, contact number, class/course details and any message you submit through our contact, enquiry or registration forms.
  • Student account data: if you create a student account to take online tests, we store your registration details, test attempts, scores and certificates so that your results can be shown to you.
  • Automatically collected data: IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages visited and time spent, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to admission, career and course enquiries.
  • To operate student accounts, online tests, results and certificates.
  • To improve website content, study material and user experience.
  • To send updates you have specifically requested.
  • To measure traffic and detect misuse or abuse of the Website.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them to remember preferences, keep you signed in to a student account, measure traffic and support advertising. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings; some features of the Website may then stop working.

4. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • More information is available in the Google advertising privacy notice and the How Google uses information from sites that use our services page.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks may use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript or web beacons in the advertisements that appear on the Website. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, the cookies used by these third parties.

5. Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the Website. These services collect aggregated, non-identifying usage data such as page views, device type and approximate location.

6. Data Retention and Security

We keep personal information only as long as it is needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent for future communications at any time. Write to info@physicaleducationcareer.in and we will respond within a reasonable period.

8. Children's Privacy

The Website is intended for students of Class 10 and above and for adult learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Third-Party Links

The Website links to university, government and other external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites; please read their own privacy policies.

10. Consent for EU/UK Visitors

Where required, advertising and analytics cookies are only set after you give consent, in line with Google's EU user consent policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
