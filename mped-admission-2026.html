Master of Physical Education Complete Guide

What is M.P.Ed?

M.P.Ed (Master of Physical Education) is a postgraduate professional degree for students who want to become lecturers, assistant professors, sports officers, researchers and high-level coaches in the field of physical education and sports.

Course Name: Master of Physical Education (M.P.Ed)
Duration: 2 Years
Eligibility: B.P.Ed Degree
Admission: Merit / Entrance Test / Physical Test

M.P.Ed Eligibility 2026

Candidates must possess a B.P.Ed degree from a recognized university. Some universities may also conduct written tests, interviews and physical fitness assessments.

  • B.P.Ed Degree Required
  • Minimum Marks as per University Rules
  • Physical Fitness Test
  • Sports Background Preferred

Admission Process

  1. Online Registration
  2. Document Verification
  3. Entrance Test (if applicable)
  4. Physical Fitness Test
  5. Merit List
  6. Final Admission

Why Choose M.P.Ed?

M.P.Ed opens opportunities in colleges, universities, sports departments, coaching centers and research institutions. It is one of the best qualifications for building a long-term career in physical education.
