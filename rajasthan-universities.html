Rajasthan Physical Education universities

This is a working directory of institutions that have PE-related pages on this site, plus names that appear in student searches. It is not a ranking and it does not list fees. A university can be genuine while a particular B.P.Ed intake is not recognised for the year you join.

Before you pay: match the exact programme name on NCTE’s public records and on that university’s prospectus. Pages for Mewar, OPJS and Madhav on this site are not treated as study guides (they are not promoted as indexable content).

How to use this list

  1. Shortlist by city and travel (Jaipur vs Alwar vs Udaipur vs Ganganagar vs Jhunjhunu).
  2. Open the checklist page if one exists — those pages tell you what to verify, not what to believe.
  3. Download the current prospectus from the university, not from this site.
  4. Ask for PFT events, internships and a fee letterhead in writing.

Course choice before city choice

  • After 12th: B.P.E.S or D.P.Ed
  • After graduation: B.P.Ed
  • After B.P.Ed: M.P.Ed
