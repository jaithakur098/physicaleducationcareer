The CBSE Physical Education syllabus, unit by unit

CBSE Physical Education is assessed as 70 marks of theory plus 30 marks of practical work in both Class 11 and Class 12. The theory syllabus is divided into ten units in each class, and the practical component covers a physical fitness test, a proficiency in games and sports, a yoga demonstration, and a practical record file with a viva voce. Knowing the split matters: many students who revise theory thoroughly still lose marks because the practical file was left to the last week.

The practical component — 30 marks you control completely

  • Physical fitness test: the prescribed battery of fitness items, recorded properly with dates and readings.
  • Proficiency in games and sports: skill demonstration in one game of your choice, with knowledge of its rules, dimensions and terminology.
  • Yoga: demonstration of the prescribed asanas with correct procedure and knowledge of their benefits.
  • Practical record file: neat, complete, with correct labelled diagrams and no copied entries.
  • Viva voce: questions on your chosen game, on the fitness test items and on the file itself.
