Physical Education admissions: choose the course, then the university

This page explains how admission usually works in Indian PE teacher-education and sports degrees. It is not an NCTE certificate and not a university prospectus. Optional WhatsApp counselling with Jai Thakur is at the bottom — universities still make the offer.

This site does not publish current fees, last dates or “India’s top NCTE colleges”. Confirm recognition for the exact programme name on NCTE’s public records and on the university notice for the session you will join.

University admission process (what you will actually do)

  1. Download this session’s prospectus. Check programme name, intake, PFT and written test.
  2. Fill the form with names matching Class 10 certificates.
  3. Sit written test and/or PFT in the published order — entrance hub.
  4. Document verification with originals.
  5. Pay only against an official university receipt after selection.

Counselling on this site can help you pick a course and a shortlist. It cannot replace the university portal.

Five checks before any fee

  • NCTE recognition for that programme and session (B.P.Ed, M.P.Ed, D.P.Ed).
  • University / UGC status as claimed on the prospectus.
  • Written fee structure on letterhead (tuition, exam, hostel, mess, one-time charges).
  • Grounds, track or courts you can visit.
  • Whether last year’s cut-off is published — if not, do not invent one.

Documents

  • Class 10 and 12 marksheets
  • Graduation / B.P.Ed as applicable
  • TC / migration as asked
  • Category, domicile, sports certificates only if claimed
  • Photos, ID, medical fitness if listed
