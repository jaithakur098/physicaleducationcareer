About the author

Physical Education Career is written and maintained by Jai Thakur, a physical education teacher who has spent his working life in classrooms, on school grounds and in admission counselling. The site began as a set of handwritten notes prepared for his own CBSE students and grew into a free resource after students from other schools, and then graduates looking for B.P.Ed and PTI guidance, began asking for the same material.

Why this site exists

Physical Education is one of the most misunderstood subjects in Indian schooling. Students are told it is an easy scoring subject, then lose marks because nobody showed them how the board expects a five-mark answer to be structured. Graduates are told a B.P.Ed leads to a government post, then discover at document verification that the college they paid was not recognised by NCTE. Both problems are solvable with accurate, freely available information, which is what this site tries to provide.

What is published here

  • Unit-wise notes for CBSE Class 11 and Class 12 Physical Education, written to match the current syllabus.
  • Previous year question papers with answer guidance and an analysis of repeated questions.
  • Online chapter tests so students can check recall rather than re-reading.
  • Course guides for B.P.Ed, M.P.Ed, B.P.E.S, D.P.Ed and Ph.D programmes.
  • Career and recruitment guidance for PET, PTI and coaching careers.
  • University information with an emphasis on checking recognition before paying fees.

How the content is prepared

Notes are written from the prescribed CBSE syllabus and standard physical education textbooks, then revised against actual board question papers so that the emphasis matches what is really asked. Admission and recruitment pages are written from official prospectuses and notifications, and readers are directed to verify the current position themselves, because rules in this field change every session. Where a page carries information that could affect a financial or career decision, it carries a note saying so.

Contact and corrections

If you find an error, an outdated rule or a broken link, please report it through the contact page. Corrections are made promptly, because a wrong eligibility rule on this site can cost a student a year. Students are welcome to write with questions about the syllabus, course selection or recruitment preparation.
