Physical Fitness, Wellness and Lifestyle

Physical fitness is the ability to perform daily activities efficiently without excessive fatigue. Wellness refers to overall physical, mental, and social well-being.

Components of Physical Fitness

  • Strength
  • Endurance
  • Flexibility
  • Speed
  • Coordination

Healthy Lifestyle Habits

  • Regular exercise
  • Balanced diet
  • Adequate sleep
  • Stress management
  • Avoidance of harmful substances
