What we can help with

This page reaches the person who writes the site, not a call centre. To get a useful reply, include your class or qualification, your state, and the exact question. Messages that simply say "send notes" are hard to answer well.

  • CBSE syllabus and notes: questions about Class 11 and Class 12 Physical Education units, answer writing and the practical file.
  • Board exam preparation: revision planning, previous year papers and chapter tests.
  • Course selection: whether B.P.Ed, M.P.Ed, B.P.E.S, D.P.Ed or a Ph.D fits your situation.
  • University questions: how to check NCTE recognition and what to verify before paying a fee.
  • Recruitment guidance: preparation for PET and PTI written and physical tests.
  • Corrections: outdated rules, broken links or errors anywhere on the site.

What we cannot do

  • We do not arrange admissions, and we are not an agent for any university or college.
  • We do not charge any fee, and we never ask for payment for information, notes or guidance.
  • We cannot confirm eligibility on behalf of a university or a recruitment board — only the official prospectus or notification can do that.
  • We cannot guarantee a seat, a job, a result or a selection.

Response time and privacy

Messages are usually answered within two to three working days, and more slowly during board examination and admission seasons. We use the details you send only to reply to your message; we do not sell, rent or publish them, and we do not add you to any marketing list. Please do not send scanned marksheets, identity documents or payment details through this page — they are not needed to answer a question. See our privacy policy for how information is handled.
