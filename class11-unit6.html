Test, Measurement and Evaluation

Testing, measurement and evaluation are important tools used to assess physical fitness, sports performance and skill development.

Definitions

  • Test: A tool used to collect information.
  • Measurement: Recording data in numerical form.
  • Evaluation: Judging performance based on collected data.

Importance

  • Measures performance
  • Tracks improvement
  • Helps in training planning
  • Identifies strengths and weaknesses
