Unit 4 – Physical Education and Sports for CWSN

Children With Special Needs (Divyangjan) – Complete CBSE Notes

Introduction

Children With Special Needs (CWSN) are children who require additional support and care due to physical, intellectual, sensory, emotional or learning disabilities. They have the same rights as other children and should be provided equal opportunities in education, sports and social activities.

Physical Education and sports play a vital role in improving the physical, mental, emotional and social development of CWSN. Through adapted physical activities and inclusive sports programmes, these children can develop confidence, independence and life skills.

Important: Inclusive education means educating all children together regardless of their abilities and disabilities.

Meaning of CWSN

CWSN stands for Children With Special Needs. These children may experience difficulties in movement, communication, learning, hearing, vision or behavior. They require specialized support to achieve their full potential.

Categories of Disabilities

1. Physical Disability

Physical disabilities affect movement and physical functioning. Examples include cerebral palsy, locomotor disability and muscular disorders.

2. Visual Impairment

Visual impairment refers to partial or complete loss of vision.

3. Hearing Impairment

Hearing impairment affects the ability to hear sounds properly.

4. Intellectual Disability

Intellectual disability affects learning ability, reasoning and adaptive behavior.

5. Learning Disability

Learning disabilities affect reading, writing, speaking or mathematical abilities.

Concept of Inclusive Education

Inclusive education is an educational approach where all students learn together in the same classroom regardless of their abilities, disabilities, social background or differences.

It ensures equal opportunities and encourages respect, cooperation and social integration among students.

Principles of Inclusive Education

  • Equal opportunities for all.
  • Respect for diversity.
  • Participation of every student.
  • Removal of barriers.
  • Supportive learning environment.
  • Individualized instruction.
  • Positive social interaction.

Need for Inclusive Education

  • Promotes equality.
  • Develops confidence among CWSN.
  • Encourages social interaction.
  • Reduces discrimination.
  • Improves learning opportunities.
  • Creates an inclusive society.
  • Builds mutual respect.

Role of Physical Education for CWSN

Physical Education helps children with special needs improve fitness, coordination, flexibility, strength and social skills. It also enhances emotional well-being and self-confidence.

  • Improves motor skills.
  • Enhances balance and coordination.
  • Develops social interaction.
  • Promotes independence.
  • Builds confidence.
  • Improves health and fitness.

Adapted Physical Education (APE)

Adapted Physical Education refers to specially designed physical education programmes that meet the unique needs of children with disabilities. Activities are modified according to the abilities and limitations of each child.

Objectives of APE

  • Improve physical fitness.
  • Develop motor skills.
  • Increase confidence.
  • Promote social participation.
  • Encourage independent living.
  • Enhance quality of life.

Benefits of Physical Activities for CWSN

  • Improves physical health.
  • Enhances muscular strength.
  • Develops coordination and balance.
  • Improves communication skills.
  • Promotes emotional stability.
  • Increases self-confidence.
  • Encourages teamwork.
  • Improves social integration.

Barriers Faced by CWSN

  • Lack of accessibility.
  • Social discrimination.
  • Insufficient sports facilities.
  • Lack of trained teachers.
  • Financial limitations.
  • Negative attitudes of society.
  • Limited awareness.

Strategies for Inclusion

  • Accessible infrastructure.
  • Modified sports equipment.
  • Teacher training programmes.
  • Individualized instruction.
  • Parental support.
  • Positive school environment.
  • Inclusive sports programmes.

Paralympic Games

The Paralympic Games are international sports competitions organized for athletes with physical disabilities. They are conducted after the Olympic Games and showcase the abilities and achievements of differently abled athletes.

Objectives of Paralympics

  • Promote equal opportunities.
  • Encourage sports participation.
  • Develop confidence among athletes.
  • Promote international friendship.
  • Celebrate sporting excellence.

Special Olympics

Special Olympics is an international movement that provides year-round sports training and athletic competitions for individuals with intellectual disabilities.

Benefits of Special Olympics

  • Improves physical fitness.
  • Develops self-esteem.
  • Encourages social interaction.
  • Promotes leadership skills.
  • Creates opportunities for success.

Deaflympics

The Deaflympics are international sporting events specifically organized for athletes with hearing impairment. These games provide opportunities for deaf athletes to compete at the highest level.
