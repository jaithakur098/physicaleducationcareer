About the Class 10 practice papers

Class 10 is the year most students first meet a full external examination pattern, and the gap between knowing the chapter and writing a good answer is at its widest here. The practice papers on this page are built around that gap. Each set follows the current CBSE structure — objective and very short answer questions, short answer questions, and long answer questions — so you practise allocating time exactly as you will have to in the real paper.

How Class 10 Physical Education and Health Education is assessed

In most CBSE schools, Class 10 Health and Physical Education is assessed internally across the year rather than through a single 80-mark external theory paper. Assessment normally covers a games and sports component, a health and fitness component, SUPW or social service, and a practical or project file. Because the weightage is spread out, consistent term-wise practice matters far more than last-minute revision.

  • Games and sports: rules, dimensions, terminology and officiating of the games chosen by your school.
  • Health and fitness: components of physical fitness, warming up and cooling down, BMI, posture, first aid and personal hygiene.
  • Yoga: common asanas, pranayama and their benefits.
  • Project and file work: neat presentation, correct diagrams and a properly maintained record of activities.

How to use these papers

  • Attempt one paper per week rather than several in one sitting; spaced practice retains far more.
  • Write full answers by hand. Reading answers creates recognition, not recall.
  • Keep a single error notebook and write down every question you got wrong, with the correct answer in one line.
  • Revise the games your school has actually selected. Rules and dimensions carry easy marks and are frequently lost.
