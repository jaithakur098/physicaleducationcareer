Rajasthan Government Jobs 2026

A clear guide to Rajasthan's major recruitment channels — RPSC, RSSB/RSMSSB, Rajasthan Police, the education department, teacher and PTI/PET posts, and sports opportunities — with eligibility, age-limit, application and preparation advice.

Home›Govt Jobs›Rajasthan Government Jobs 2026

Rajasthan runs one of the largest state recruitment calendars in India. Lakhs of candidates appear each year for posts ranging from clerks and sub-inspectors to teachers, PTI/PET instructors and university staff. For Physical Education students, the state is especially important because school and departmental PTI/PET posts are recruited here regularly. This page explains the main bodies, the typical process and how to prepare — without inventing any vacancy, date or salary figure.

Read this first: This page is guidance only. Vacancies, eligibility, exam pattern, age limits, physical standards and last dates exist only in the official notification PDF published by the concerned Rajasthan body. Always verify there before applying.

Major Rajasthan Recruiting Bodies

RPSC (Rajasthan Public Service Commission)

RPSC conducts recruitment for state services, subordinate services, school lecturers, assistant professors and various departmental posts. Notifications, syllabi and admit cards are published on the commission's official website.

RSSB / RSMSSB (Rajasthan Staff Selection Board)

The Staff Selection Board handles a large volume of non-gazetted posts — clerks, technicians, foresters, and importantly PTI/PET and other school-staff recruitments when notified. This is the body most PE candidates watch.

Rajasthan Police

Constable and sub-inspector recruitments are conducted by the police department. These involve a written paper and a physical-efficiency test; a fitness background helps but the official standards are what matter.

Rajasthan Education Department

Government school teacher recruitment — PRT, TGT, PGT, PTI and PET — falls under the school/education department. The exact qualification, eligibility test and selection method are given in each advertisement.

Rajasthan Universities

State universities recruit teaching and non-teaching staff through their own notifications. PE and sports posts in universities follow the UGC/NCTE framework where applicable.

Physical Education & Sports Opportunities in Rajasthan

Rajasthan offers several routes for PE candidates: school PTI/PET posts through the Staff Selection Board, Physical Education Teacher and sports-coach positions in government schools and bodies, and sports-quota openings where achievement certificates carry weight. A D.P.Ed, B.P.Ed, M.P.Ed or B.P.E.S degree, with NCTE recognition for teacher-education programmes, is the usual academic base.

For the physical stage, train for the events and qualifying marks named in the notification — never guess them from old papers. Our PTI physical + written outline and CBSE unit notes are useful for theory. Also read our dedicated guidance on how government PTI selection works and handling Rajasthan PTI rumours.

Eligibility, Age Limit & Application Process

Eligibility

Eligibility is post-specific. A clerk post may need 12th or graduation; a PTI post needs the PE qualification named; a lecturer post needs a master's with NET/SET as specified. Read the "Essential Qualification" clause carefully — it overrides any general rule.

Age Limit

Minimum and maximum age, and any relaxation for reserved categories, domicile or women, are printed in the notification. Do not assume a single fixed age; check the clause for your category.

Application Process

  • Visit the official portal named in the advertisement.
  • Register with a valid email and mobile number.
  • Fill the form, upload documents in the required size/format.
  • Pay the fee through the official payment gateway and save the receipt.
  • Download and keep the confirmation/registration slip.

Selection Process

Typically a written exam, sometimes a physical-efficiency test (for police/PTI/PET), document verification and, for some posts, an interview or demo. The scheme of examination — papers, marks, negative marking — is given in the notification.

CET, State Examinations & Documents

For certain group posts the state uses a Common Eligibility Test to shortlist candidates before the departmental exam. Whether a post uses CET, and its weightage, is decided by the state and printed in the notification — do not treat CET as applicable to every post.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • 10th marksheet (date of birth) and 12th marksheet.
  • Graduation and PE degree / diploma certificates.
  • NCTE recognition printout for your teacher-education programme year.
  • Caste / EWS / PwD certificates in the format the notice names.
  • Domicile certificate where required.
  • Sports certificates with verification stamps, if applying under sports considerations.
  • Recent photos and a valid photo ID matching your application name.

Preparation Strategy

  • Build a daily plan covering Rajasthan GK, the subject paper and (if applicable) the physical test.
  • Revise PE theory: fitness, yoga, anatomy at teacher depth, first aid, school-game rules, intramural organisation.
  • Practice previous papers only as a pattern guide — the next paper may differ.
  • Maintain fitness year-round so the PET stage is not a last-minute scramble.
  • Use PET preparation resources and teaching interview questions for the interview/demo stage.
Common mistake: Treating a social-media "vacancy screenshot" or a dated URL as proof. The only valid source is the official RPSC/RSSB/ department advertisement on its own website.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which bodies conduct Rajasthan government recruitment?

Major recruiters include the Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC), the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB/RSMSSB), Rajasthan Police, the state education department and various universities. The exact body is named in each official notification.

Is a Rajasthan domicile required for all state posts?

Not always. Some posts are open to all Indian citizens, while others give reservation or preference to Rajasthan domicile holders. The notification's domicile and reservation clause is final.

What is CET in Rajasthan recruitment?

The state conducts a Common Eligibility Test for certain group posts to shortlist candidates before the departmental exam. Whether a post uses CET is decided by the state and printed in the notification.

How can a Physical Education candidate get a Rajasthan government job?

Through PTI/PET and Physical Education Teacher posts issued by the education department or boards, plus sports-quota openings. Keep D.P.Ed/B.P.Ed/M.P.Ed certificates, NCTE recognition and sports certificates ready.

Where do I apply for Rajasthan government jobs?

Only through the official portal named in the notification. RPSC and RSSB publish advertisements on their own official websites; apply there and keep the registration number safe.

What is the usual age limit?

Age limits vary by post and category and are printed in the notification. Reserved-category relaxation, if any, is also given there. Do not assume a fixed number.

How should I prepare for Rajasthan teacher and PTI exams?

Revise Rajasthan general knowledge, PE theory, pedagogy, Hindi/English and the physical-efficiency syllabus. Use our PET preparation guide and CBSE unit notes for structured practice.

How do I avoid Rajasthan recruitment rumours?

Treat any date, vacancy or pattern as valid only after an official RPSC/RSSB advertisement. Social-media screenshots are not proof. See our dedicated page on handling PTI rumours.

Does Physical Education Career issue Rajasthan job offers?

No. We are an education and guidance platform, not a government department. We do not issue offer letters or confirm seats.
