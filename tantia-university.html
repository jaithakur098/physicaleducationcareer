About Tantia University

Tantia University is a private university located in Sri Ganganagar, Rajasthan. The university offers programmes in Medical Sciences, Allied Health Sciences, Agriculture, Pharmacy, Education, Management and Physical Education. The Faculty of Physical Education focuses on practical training, sports performance, fitness education and professional teacher preparation.

Students from Rajasthan and other states choose Tantia University for its modern campus, sports infrastructure, experienced faculty and professional Physical Education programmes.

Eligibility Criteria

  • D.P.Ed. – Candidates should have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination as per the latest university admission rules.
  • B.P.E.S. – Candidates should have completed 10+2 from a recognised board.
  • B.P.Ed. – Candidates should possess a Bachelor's Degree with the required percentage as per NCTE and university norms.
  • M.P.Ed. – Candidates should have completed B.P.Ed. from a recognised university.
  • Admission eligibility may change according to the latest university notification.
