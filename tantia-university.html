Tantia University, Sri Ganganagar

Tantia University is a private university in Sri Ganganagar, in the far north of Rajasthan, near the Punjab and Haryana borders. The location is the main decision factor: travel from Jaipur or Udaipur is a long haul; the campus makes more sense if your home district is Ganganagar, Hanumangarh or nearby Punjab towns.

Official site listed in earlier campus notes: tantiauniversity.com. Confirm whether B.P.Ed, M.P.Ed and/or D.P.Ed are actually offered this session. Do not assume a diploma exists because a blog listed it last year.

Programmes to look up (not a promise)

Older pages on this site mentioned B.P.Ed, M.P.Ed and D.P.Ed in connection with Tantia. Treat that as a search list for the prospectus, not as current inventory.

  • B.P.Ed — graduation route
  • D.P.Ed — diploma after 12th; duration and NCTE status only from the notice
  • M.P.Ed

Questions that are specific to Ganganagar

  • Hostel mess and climate (extreme summer) — ask current students, not the brochure.
  • How internships are arranged if practice schools are far from campus.
  • Whether PFT is on campus or at a city stadium.

Compare only with similar distance choices

Do not compare Tantia’s travel cost with Alwar or Jaipur as if they were the same commute. If you need a Jaipur campus, start with Uniraj or NIMS.
