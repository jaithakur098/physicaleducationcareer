About JJT University

Shri Jagdishprasad Jhabarmal Tibrewala University (JJT University), located in Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan, is a private university established under the Rajasthan State Legislature. The university offers programmes across multiple disciplines, including Physical Education. Students can pursue D.P.Ed., B.P.Ed., M.P.Ed. and doctoral studies, subject to the programmes offered in the relevant admission session.

The university focuses on academic learning, practical training, sports participation and skill development. Students are encouraged to participate in sports competitions, research activities and professional development programmes to build careers in teaching, coaching and sports management.

Department of Physical Education

The Department of Physical Education at Shri JJT University offers professional education in sports science, coaching, physical fitness and teacher education. The department provides practical training along with classroom teaching and has facilities for athletics, basketball, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, lawn tennis, hockey, cricket and indigenous games. M.P.Ed. Programme

The Master of Physical Education (M.P.Ed.) programme provides advanced learning in sports science, coaching, research methodology, biomechanics, exercise physiology and physical education. It is intended for graduates who want careers in higher education, coaching and research. [oai_citation:3‡Shri JJT University](https://www.jjtu.ac.in/page/80)
