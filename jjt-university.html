Shri Jagdishprasad Jhabarmal Tibrewala University (JJT), Jhunjhunu

JJT is a private university in Jhunjhunu district, Shekhawati. For PE students the practical question is travel from Jaipur/Delhi versus living on campus, and whether athletics and games facilities match what the university describes on its own PE page.

Do not treat aggregator sites as the prospectus. Use jjtu.ac.in. The university has published a Physical Education page at jjtu.ac.in/page/80 — read it for the current programme list. This site does not copy fee tables or placement lists from third-party portals.

What the university itself has described

On its PE page, JJT has described an Institute / Department of Physical Education with outdoor and indoor games and a 400-metre athletics track. Treat that as a claim to verify on a visit: walk the track, check whether it is usable in monsoon, and ask who books it for PE practicals versus general campus use.

The university has also published a B.P.Ed syllabus PDF in the past. Syllabus files go out of date. Ask for the syllabus attached to this admission session.

Programmes to confirm on the official PE page

  • B.P.Ed (teacher education — NCTE check is essential)
  • B.P.E.S (undergraduate sports degree — different from B.P.Ed)
  • M.P.Ed
  • Ph.D in Physical Education where offered

Guides: B.P.Ed · B.P.E.S · M.P.Ed · Ph.D.

Jhunjhunu comparison

Singhania University is also in Jhunjhunu district (Pacheri Bari). Do not mix the two application portals. Visit both only if you are comparing hostels, PFT dates and NCTE files side by side.

Visit checklist unique to this campus

  • Confirm the PE office location (not only the main gate reception).
  • Ask whether internship / teaching practice schools are listed in writing.
  • Ask for a written fee structure; ignore informal WhatsApp quotes.
