Rajasthan PTI recruitment: use RSSB, not rumours

The URL of this page still contains “2027” because it was first published during social-media talk of a 2027 exam. That is not an official date. Vacancies, eligibility, exam pattern, physical standards and last date exist only in a Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) advertisement (or whichever body the state names in that PDF).

Do not treat “about 900 posts”, “April 2027” or any private portal calendar as information. When a notification is out, download it from the official board website and ignore this page’s filename.

What you can usefully do before a notification

  1. Keep fitness at PET-event level (sprint, endurance, jump, throw — exact battery comes later) — physical + written outline.
  2. Revise PE theory: Rajasthan GK is often a separate paper or section in past recruitments, but the next paper may differ. PE content: unit notes plus your D.P.Ed/B.P.Ed notes.
  3. Collect sports certificates with proper stamps.
  4. Print NCTE recognition for your qualification.

Eligibility — only a reminder

Past Rajasthan PTI notices have typically required 10+2 plus C.P.Ed / D.P.Ed / B.P.Ed as specified. The next notice can change that. See D.P.Ed and B.P.Ed for what those courses are, not for this year’s legal eligibility.

When the PDF is published, read these lines first

  • Post name and department
  • Essential qualification and NCTE wording
  • Age on the stated date
  • Scheme of examination (papers, marks, negative marking)
  • Physical efficiency table (men/women, events, qualifying vs merit)
  • Document list and photo specifications
  • Application fee and portal URL

Job description: PET career. General government process: government PTI.
