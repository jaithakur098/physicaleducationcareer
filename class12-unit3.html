Unit 3 – Yoga as Preventive Measure for Lifestyle Disease

Complete CBSE Notes, MCQs, Important Questions & Exam Preparation

Introduction to Yoga

Yoga is one of the oldest systems of physical, mental and spiritual development. It originated in ancient India and has been practiced for thousands of years. The word Yoga is derived from the Sanskrit word "Yuj" which means union. Yoga aims to unite the body, mind and soul for complete well-being.

In modern society, lifestyle diseases are increasing rapidly because of stress, unhealthy eating habits, lack of physical activity, obesity and poor daily routines. Yoga provides an effective natural solution to prevent and control these diseases.

Exam Point: Yoga is considered one of the best preventive measures for lifestyle diseases because it improves physical fitness, mental health and emotional stability.

Meaning of Yoga

Yoga is a scientific discipline that combines physical postures (Asanas), breathing techniques (Pranayama), meditation and relaxation methods to achieve complete health and harmony.

Yoga is not only a form of exercise but also a way of living that promotes balance between physical health, mental peace and spiritual growth.

Importance of Yoga

  • Improves flexibility and balance.
  • Enhances muscular strength.
  • Reduces stress and anxiety.
  • Improves concentration and memory.
  • Promotes emotional stability.
  • Improves respiratory efficiency.
  • Boosts immunity.
  • Maintains overall fitness.
  • Supports healthy lifestyle habits.
  • Helps prevent chronic diseases.

What are Lifestyle Diseases?

Lifestyle diseases are diseases that occur mainly because of unhealthy lifestyle habits such as physical inactivity, poor diet, smoking, alcohol consumption, obesity and chronic stress.

These diseases develop gradually over time and affect the quality of life. Most lifestyle diseases can be prevented by healthy habits and regular physical activity.

Common Lifestyle Diseases

  • Obesity
  • Diabetes Mellitus
  • Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)
  • Asthma
  • Coronary Heart Disease
  • Back Pain
  • Stress Disorders

Causes of Lifestyle Diseases

  • Lack of physical activity.
  • Excessive consumption of junk food.
  • Stress and anxiety.
  • Obesity.
  • Smoking and tobacco use.
  • Alcohol consumption.
  • Poor sleeping habits.
  • Unhealthy daily routine.

Role of Yoga in Prevention of Lifestyle Diseases

Regular practice of Yoga improves circulation, respiration, digestion and mental health. It helps reduce body fat, improves flexibility and controls stress levels. Yoga also strengthens internal organs and improves overall efficiency of body systems.

  • Controls obesity.
  • Reduces stress hormones.
  • Improves heart health.
  • Regulates blood pressure.
  • Improves blood sugar control.
  • Enhances lung capacity.
  • Improves sleep quality.

Yoga for Obesity

Obesity is the excessive accumulation of body fat due to overeating, lack of physical activity and unhealthy lifestyle habits. Obesity increases the risk of heart disease, diabetes and hypertension.

Benefits of Yoga for Obesity

  • Helps burn extra calories.
  • Improves metabolism.
  • Reduces body fat.
  • Improves digestion.
  • Enhances flexibility.

Recommended Yogasanas

  • Tadasana
  • Trikonasana
  • Bhujangasana
  • Paschimottanasana
  • Pavanmuktasana

Yoga for Diabetes

Diabetes is a metabolic disorder in which blood sugar levels become abnormally high. Regular Yoga practice helps regulate insulin function and improve blood sugar control.

Benefits of Yoga for Diabetes

  • Improves insulin sensitivity.
  • Reduces stress levels.
  • Controls blood glucose.
  • Improves circulation.
  • Supports weight management.

Recommended Yogasanas

  • Bhujangasana
  • Mandukasana
  • Ardha Matsyendrasana
  • Vajrasana
  • Paschimottanasana

Yoga for Hypertension

Hypertension or high blood pressure is a condition where blood pressure remains consistently above normal levels. Yoga helps relax the nervous system and maintain healthy blood pressure.

Benefits of Yoga for Hypertension

  • Reduces stress and anxiety.
  • Lowers blood pressure.
  • Improves heart health.
  • Promotes relaxation.
  • Enhances circulation.

Recommended Yogasanas

  • Shavasana
  • Sukhasana
  • Vajrasana
  • Makarasana
  • Tadasana

Yoga for Asthma

Asthma is a respiratory disorder that causes breathing difficulties due to inflammation and narrowing of airways. Yoga improves lung function and breathing efficiency.

Benefits of Yoga for Asthma

  • Improves lung capacity.
  • Strengthens respiratory muscles.
  • Reduces breathing difficulties.
  • Improves oxygen intake.
  • Promotes relaxation.

Recommended Yogasanas

  • Bhujangasana
  • Matsyasana
  • Gomukhasana
  • Sukhasana
  • Parvatasana

Important Pranayama Techniques

  • Anulom Vilom
  • Kapalbhati
  • Bhramari
  • Nadi Shodhana
  • Ujjayi Pranayama

Pranayama improves respiratory efficiency, reduces stress and enhances overall health.

Yoga is a system of physical, mental and spiritual practices that promote overall well-being.

What are lifestyle diseases?

Lifestyle diseases are diseases caused mainly by unhealthy habits and lack of physical activity.

Can Yoga prevent diabetes?

Yoga helps regulate blood sugar levels and supports diabetes management.
