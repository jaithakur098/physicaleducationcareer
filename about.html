About Physical Education Career

Welcome to Physical Education Career, India's dedicated platform for Physical Education students, teachers, coaches and sports professionals.

Physical Education Career was founded by Jai Thakur (NIS, M.P.Ed.) with the aim of providing accurate information related to B.P.Ed., M.P.Ed., D.P.Ed., Physical Education careers, PTI recruitment, CBSE Physical Education studies and sports education.

Founder Profile

Jai Thakur is a Physical Education professional, Taekwondo coach and educator with experience in teaching, sports training and career guidance. Over the years he has guided hundreds of students in physical education courses, sports participation and career planning.

Qualifications

  • Master of Physical Education (M.P.Ed.)
  • Bachelor of Physical Education (B.P.Ed.)
  • NIS Certified Coach
  • 3rd Dan Black Belt in Taekwondo
  • National Referee
  • Rajasthan Team Coach

Our Mission

Our mission is to make quality Physical Education information accessible to every student in India. We aim to provide:

  • D.P.Ed. course details
  • PTI recruitment updates
  • CBSE Physical Education notes
  • Previous year question papers

  • Coaching and fitness knowledge

What We Offer

Admission Guidance

We provide information about admissions in B.P.Ed., M.P.Ed., D.P.Ed. and other Physical Education programmes across India. See our admissions page and university list.

Study Materials

Students can access notes, practice questions, quizzes and previous year papers for academic preparation on our CBSE Physical Education and downloads pages.

Career Counselling

We help students understand the career opportunities available after completing Physical Education courses — see the PET career guide.

Recruitment Updates

Regular updates related to PTI recruitment, teaching jobs and sports-related vacancies are shared on the platform.

Editorial Approach

Every admission, syllabus and recruitment page is written from official university notifications, CBSE curriculum documents and government recruitment advertisements, and is reviewed by Jai Thakur before publishing. When a date or rule is likely to change, we say so and point readers to the official source.
