About Physical Education Career

Physical Education Career is an independent educational website at physicaleducationcareer.in. It is operated from Alwar, Rajasthan by Jai Thakur. The site is not a university, not a coaching franchise, and not an official CBSE, NCTE or government portal.

Who operates the website

Jai Thakur is a Physical Education professional and Taekwondo coach. Qualifications and roles already published on this site are:

  • B.P.Ed.
  • M.P.Ed.
  • NIS Taekwondo Coach
  • Physical Education Teacher
  • Sports Coordinator
  • Tournament organiser and sports event manager
  • Associated with Ultimate Taekwondo Academy, Alwar

We do not list honorary titles, rankings, placement percentages or institutional affiliations that are not already documented on this website.

What the website provides

  • CBSE Class 11 and Class 12 Physical Education notes, questions and previous-year paper links
  • Guidance on D.P.Ed, B.P.E.S, B.P.Ed, M.P.Ed and Ph.D pathways
  • University pages for selected Rajasthan institutions, with a verification checklist rather than invented fees or rankings
  • PET / PTI career orientation
  • A public tournament portal for the Alwar Cup Taekwondo Championship (registration, draws, ID cards and certificates are separate tools, not study content)
  • Student practice tests that require a login

How educational information is prepared

Study notes are written as classroom-style explanations for board and entrance preparation. They are original summaries, not copies of any textbook. Admission and recruitment pages describe processes that students typically face (documents, fitness tests, NCTE checks). Where a fee, date, ranking, recognition status or vacancy figure cannot be confirmed from an official source in front of us, we say so and ask you to verify it.

Read the Editorial Policy for how corrections are handled.

How to report incorrect information

Email info@physicaleducationcareer.in with the page URL, the sentence that is wrong, and the official source (prospectus, NCTE listing, board circular). Phone and WhatsApp: +91 98283 20688.

How information is updated

Pages carry a last-updated note where it is useful. University and recruitment details change every session. We update pages when a reader reports an error or when we re-check a prospectus. Until then, treat official university and government websites as the source of record.
