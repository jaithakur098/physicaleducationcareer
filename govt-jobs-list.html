How This Aggregator Works

  • We collect post names as discovery references and then verify the factual details from the official recruitment notification.
  • Each job opens a dedicated detail page with organization, post, eligibility, age limit, fee, dates, selection, salary, documents and official links — all marked “Verify from official notification” until confirmed.
  • Status badges (Active, Last Date Today, Upcoming, Closed, Result Out) are set only from verified dates. Sample entries show a “Sample” badge.
  • Official Notification and Apply Online links are added only after verification; we never invent URLs.
