Taekwondo coaching in a school setting

School taekwondo is not a full combat club session squeezed into 35 minutes. Contact, protective equipment and the age of the group decide everything. The notes below come from school and academy work; they are not a substitute for your national federation’s instructor rules.

Class structure (40 minutes)

  1. Bow, attendance, jewellery check (5 min).
  2. Pulse-raising and joint mobility; then kicks on the spot without a partner (8 min).
  3. Technique of the day on pads or a bag if available (12 min).
  4. Poomsae / pattern practice or a non-contact game (10 min).
  5. Stretch, reminder of next grading item (5 min).

Safety non-negotiables

  • No full-contact sparring in a crowded school hall without mats, head guards, and a qualified supervisor ratio.
  • Kicks to the head are a demonstration skill, not a lunch-break activity.
  • Water and a stop rule for dizziness.

Grading

School “belts” that are not registered with a recognised body confuse parents. If you run a school module, call it a skill level (1–4) unless you are actually affiliated and using official kup/dan procedures.

From school demo to championship

A championship needs weigh-in, categories, a draw, and medical cover. The public side of one such event is the Alwar Cup portal. Organisation theory: tournament checklist.
