School sports event formats that fit a one-day meet

Choose the format from the number of entries and the number of officials, not from what a television final looks like.

Track: heats to a final

If you have 32 runners for 100 m and 8 lanes, run 4 heats. Take the first 2 from each heat (8) to a final, or take winners plus fastest losers if you have a photo-finish or reliable hand timing. Publish the qualification rule on the notice board before the first heat.

Relays

4×100 m needs baton practice. If classes have never exchanged a baton, run a 4×50 m shuttle relay on a marked 50 m to reduce dropped-baton chaos. Count a drop as a disqualification only if you announced it.

Field events

Give each athlete 3 trials in long jump or throws. Call names in a fixed order. Do not allow a fourth “practice” trial once the competition has started.

Team games: knock-out vs league

Knock-out: matches = teams − 1. Fast, but a good team can go out after one bad morning. League in pools: two pools of 4, then winners play a final. More matches, fairer, needs more time. Formulae: Class 12 Unit 1.

Modified games for mass participation

  • 5-a-side football on a shortened pitch, 8-minute halves, rolling substitutes.
  • 3-on-3 basketball to 11 points or 8 minutes.
  • Throwball / volleyball best of 1 set to 15 if you have 12 teams and one court.

These are organisational formats. Playing laws for a recognised championship must come from the sport’s official body.
