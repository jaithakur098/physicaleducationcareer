Entrance preparation for PE courses

Most PE admissions combine a written paper (or merit), a physical fitness test, and document verification. The exact battery changes by university. Train the components that appear repeatedly; do not memorise last year’s cut-off as if it were a law.

Written paper — typical topic groups

University papers often sample school PE knowledge plus general sports awareness. Use Class 11–12 notes as a base, then add sports current affairs from official federation sites, not social media rumours.

  • History, aims and career options in PE — Class 11 Unit 1
  • Yoga — Class 11 Unit 3
  • Test and measurement — Class 12 Unit 6
  • Training methods — Class 12 Unit 10
  • Event management and fixtures — Class 12 Unit 1

Documents the office usually asks to see

  • Photo ID matching Class 10 spelling
  • Marksheets for the last qualifying exam
  • Sports certificates only if the notice gives marks
  • Medical fitness if listed

Physical fitness test training guide

Before you pay a fee, confirm that the specific programme is recognised for that session. Start at the university admission notice and the NCTE public portal. This site does not certify colleges.
