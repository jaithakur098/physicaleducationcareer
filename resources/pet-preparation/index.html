PET and PTI preparation

PET (Physical Education Teacher) and PTI (Physical Training Instructor) recruitments are notification-specific. Running 800 metres in 2:40 might be required in one advertisement and not mentioned in another. Print the current advertisement and train to that table.

Written syllabus that usually overlaps PE degrees

  • History and foundations of PE
  • Anatomy, physiology, kinesiology at school-teacher depth
  • Sports training and periodisation in simple language
  • Officiating, organisation of intramurals
  • Yoga and health education
  • Rules of major school games at “explain to a class” level

Physical events — do not invent a battery

Until the advertisement is out, maintain general PE fitness: a sprint, a middle-distance run, a jump and a throw. The day the PDF is published, switch to those distances and those qualifying times. Injuries from copying a YouTube “PTI PET” video that does not match your notification are common.

Use CBSE unit notes as a revision spine, then add the state textbook or degree notes named in the advertisement.
