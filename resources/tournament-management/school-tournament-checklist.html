School tournament checklist

Use this as an operational list. Theory of committees is in Class 12 Unit 1.

Three weeks before

  • Written permission from the principal; date that does not clash with exams.
  • Name the organising secretary and the chief referee.
  • Budget: balls, lime/tape, certificates, water, first-aid restock, officials’ tea.
  • Circular to classes with events, age groups, last date of entry.

Ten days before

  • Close entries. Number the teams. Draw fixtures in public (or publish the seeding rule).
  • Book the ground; check pits, posts, nets.
  • Confirm a first-aid person and a vehicle plan for hospital.
  • Print score sheets with event name, round, and signature line.

Match day

  • Ground committee marks courts before the first assembly.
  • Officials committee briefs umpires for 10 minutes: timing, protest rule, dissent.
  • Results committee writes winners in ink; no overwriting without an initial.
  • Water and shade; stop play if lightning or extreme heat as per school policy.

After the event

  • Prize list signed by the principal.
  • Accounts: receipts attached.
  • One-page report: number of participants, injuries (if any), what to change next year.

Club-scale software example: Alwar Cup public portal (draws, results, certificate verify). Registration for that event is a separate coach workflow.
