Tournament management

A tournament fails for operational reasons: missing officials, unmarked courts, no first-aid, or a draw that nobody understands. Theory for this sits in Class 12 Unit 1 — Management of sporting events. Practice sits in the checklist below.

  • School tournament checklist
  • Event formats
  • CBSE tournament information page
  • Alwar Cup — public results, draws and verification

Committees you can name in a viva

Pre-event: publicity, reception, grounds, equipment, first aid, finance. During: officials, marshalling, results. After: prize, press, accounts. Assign names on paper the week before; a checklist without names fails at 7 a.m. on event day.

Coach login, fixture generation, ID cards and certificates are internal tools. They are not study articles. Coaches use tournament-coach.html after they have an account; that page should not be treated as a content landing page.
