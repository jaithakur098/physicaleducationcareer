How to mark yourself

Question typeWhat earns marks
1 markThe precise term (for example “bye”, not “rest day”).
3 marksThree distinct points, each with a short explanation.
5 marksDefinition, classification or procedure, one example, one diagram or fixture if asked.

A 90-minute marking session

Pick one 5-mark fixture or deformity question from Class 12 PYQ. Draw it once, then redraw from memory. That is more useful than collecting unmarked PDFs. B.P.Ed students: semester II papers are practice files, not your university’s official paper.
