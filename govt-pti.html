Government PTI Jobs 2026 – Complete Guide

Government Physical Training Instructor (PTI) jobs are among the most sought-after opportunities for Physical Education graduates in India. PTI teachers are recruited in government schools through various state and central recruitment agencies.

Eligibility for Government PTI Jobs

  • Senior Secondary (10+2) or Graduation as per recruitment rules.
  • D.P.Ed., B.P.Ed. or equivalent Physical Education qualification.
  • Knowledge of sports and physical education activities.
  • Age limit generally 18–40 years (varies by state).

Major PTI Recruitments

  • Rajasthan PTI Recruitment
  • Haryana PTI Recruitment
  • Punjab PTI Recruitment
  • Delhi Physical Education Teacher Recruitment
  • KVS Physical Education Teacher Recruitment
  • NVS Physical Education Teacher Recruitment

Selection Process

The selection process usually includes a written examination followed by document verification. Some states may also conduct skill tests or sports achievement evaluation.

Salary of Government PTI

Government PTI teachers generally receive salaries according to state government pay scales. Starting salaries usually range from ₹25,000 to ₹60,000+ per month depending on the post and state.

Preparation Tips

  • Study Rajasthan GK and Current Affairs.
  • Prepare Physical Education theory thoroughly.
  • Practice previous year question papers.
  • Focus on sports rules and training methods.
  • Regularly solve mock tests.

Useful Resources

Candidates should regularly check official recruitment notifications and continue practicing previous year question papers for better preparation.

Why Choose Physical Education as a Career?

Physical Education offers excellent career opportunities in schools, colleges, sports academies, fitness industries and government departments. Qualified professionals can work as PTI teachers, coaches, trainers and sports administrators.
