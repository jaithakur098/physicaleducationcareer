Government PTI and PET recruitment

A government Physical Training Instructor / PE teacher post is filled through a published advertisement. Nobody can sell you the post. If someone asks for money to “confirm a seat”, walk away.

This page does not quote monthly salary figures. Pay is whatever the official pay scale in that notification says. Age limits, vacancies and physical standards also come only from that PDF.

Who issues notifications (examples, not a live list)

  • State recruiting boards / public service commissions (Rajasthan candidates: start at RSSB when a PTI advertisement exists — how to treat rumours)
  • Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (PET)
  • Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti
  • Sainik Schools, APS and other systems with their own boards

Eligibility pattern (always overridden by the notice)

  • Schooling + D.P.Ed / C.P.Ed / B.P.Ed / M.P.Ed as named
  • NCTE recognition for teacher-education degrees
  • TET only if required
  • Domicile / category rules if printed

Course pages: D.P.Ed · B.P.Ed · M.P.Ed.

Typical stages

  1. Written paper — PE theory, teaching, GK / state GK, sometimes language. Most people fail here.
  2. Physical test — events and qualifying marks from the notification only. Training plan: physical + written outline.
  3. Sports certificate weightage — only originals, only if the notice gives marks.
  4. Document verification — degree, NCTE, category, photos matching certificates.
  5. Interview / demo — common in KVS/NVS-style recruitments; school private jobs always have a demo — questions.

Documents folder to keep ready

  • 10th (DOB), 12th, graduation, PE diploma/degree
  • NCTE recognition printout for your programme year
  • Category / EWS / PwD as applicable, in the format the notice names
  • Sports certificates with verification stamps where required
  • Application fee receipt and registration number

Written topics that overlap PE degrees

History of PE, fitness components, training methods, yoga, first aid, rules of school games, anatomy at teacher depth, organisation of intramurals. Use CBSE units plus the syllabus printed in the advertisement.

Day-to-day job: PET career. Injury awareness: injury prevention.
