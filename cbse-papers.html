How to practise

  • Read the unit, then write, then mark. Cold papers without notes teach panic, not structure.
  • Time the 70-mark theory paper.
  • High-weightage writing: fixtures, yoga for lifestyle diseases, deformities, nutrition, injuries, Newton’s laws in sport — still verify against this year’s sample paper.
