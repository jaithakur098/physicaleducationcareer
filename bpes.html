B.P.E.S Admission 2026: Complete Guide

B.P.E.S (Bachelor of Physical Education and Sports) ek undergraduate degree course hai jo sports, fitness, coaching aur physical education ke field me career banana chahne wale students ke liye design kiya gaya hai. Yeh course students ko sports science, coaching methods, physical fitness aur sports management ke baare me detailed knowledge pradan karta hai.

B.P.E.S Full Form

B.P.E.S ka full form Bachelor of Physical Education and Sports hai. Yeh ek professional undergraduate degree hai jo sports education aur fitness industry me career opportunities provide karti hai.

What is B.P.E.S?

B.P.E.S course ka uddeshya students ko sports coaching, fitness training, sports science aur physical education ke modern concepts se parichit karana hai. Is course me theory aur practical dono ka saman mahatva hota hai.

Course Duration

B.P.E.S generally 3 se 4 years ka undergraduate program hota hai. Duration university aur curriculum ke anusaar alag ho sakti hai.

Eligibility Criteria

  • 10+2 Pass from a recognized board.
  • Minimum 45%–50% marks.
  • Sports participation certificates beneficial ho sakte hain.
  • Physical fitness test kuch institutions me required ho sakta hai.

Admission Process

  1. Application Form Submission
  2. Merit List / Entrance Test
  3. Physical Fitness Assessment
  4. Document Verification
  5. Final Admission
