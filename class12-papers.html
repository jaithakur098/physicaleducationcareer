How to sit a paper

  1. 70 marks theory: time yourself. Leave diagrams until you have listed points.
  2. Mark against syllabus headings, not against a friend’s length.
  3. 30 marks practical: file and viva are separate — see practical hub.

High-yield writing (not a prediction)

In recent papers, fixtures, postural deformities, asanas for lifestyle diseases, nutrition, injuries and Newton’s laws in sport have appeared often. That is a revision priority list, not a leaked paper.
