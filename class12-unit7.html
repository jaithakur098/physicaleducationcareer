Unit 7: Physiology and Sports Injuries

Physiology is the branch of science that studies the functions of the human body. Sports physiology focuses on how exercise affects body systems and helps improve athletic performance. Understanding physiology helps athletes train effectively and prevent injuries.

Meaning of Physiology

Physiology is the study of the functions and activities of living organisms and their body parts. It explains how different systems of the body work together.

Importance of Physiology in Sports

  • Improves sports performance.
  • Helps in training planning.
  • Enhances recovery.
  • Prevents injuries.
  • Maintains fitness and health.

Effects of Exercise on Muscular System

  • Increase in muscle size.
  • Increase in muscle strength.
  • Improved muscular endurance.
  • Better blood supply to muscles.
  • Reduced fatigue.

Effects of Exercise on Cardiovascular System

  • Improved heart efficiency.
  • Increased stroke volume.
  • Better blood circulation.
  • Reduced resting heart rate.
  • Improved oxygen transport.

Effects of Exercise on Respiratory System

  • Increased lung capacity.
  • Improved oxygen intake.
  • Enhanced breathing efficiency.
  • Better respiratory endurance.

Sports Injuries

Sports injuries are injuries that occur during sports activities, exercise or physical training. Proper warm-up, technique and equipment can reduce the risk of injuries.

Types of Sports Injuries

  • Soft Tissue Injuries
  • Bone Injuries
  • Joint Injuries
  • Muscle Injuries

Sprain

A sprain is an injury to a ligament caused by overstretching or tearing.

Symptoms

  • Pain
  • Swelling
  • Bruising
  • Difficulty in movement

Strain

A strain is an injury to a muscle or tendon caused by excessive stretching or overuse.

Symptoms

  • Pain
  • Muscle weakness
  • Swelling
  • Limited movement

Dislocation

Dislocation occurs when bones in a joint are forced out of their normal position.

Fracture

A fracture is a break or crack in a bone caused by excessive force or trauma.

Common Sports Injuries

  • Ankle Sprain
  • Hamstring Strain
  • Knee Injury
  • Shoulder Dislocation
  • Fractures

First Aid for Sports Injuries

Immediate treatment can reduce the severity of injuries and speed up recovery.

RICE Method

  • R - Rest
  • I - Ice
  • C - Compression
  • E - Elevation

Prevention of Sports Injuries

  • Proper warm-up.
  • Use correct techniques.
  • Wear appropriate equipment.
  • Maintain fitness.
  • Take adequate rest.
  • Follow training principles.

Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation is the process of restoring normal function after an injury. It includes exercise, physiotherapy and gradual return to sports activities.

Importance of Rehabilitation

  • Restores strength.
  • Improves flexibility.
  • Prevents re-injury.
  • Speeds recovery.

Conclusion

Physiology helps athletes understand how the body responds to exercise and training. Knowledge of sports injuries, their prevention and treatment helps athletes maintain performance and reduce the risk of long-term health problems.
