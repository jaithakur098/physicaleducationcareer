Editorial Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

This policy explains how we write and maintain pages on physicaleducationcareer.in. It exists so students, parents and teachers can judge whether a page is suitable to rely on.

Purpose of the site

The site exists to help students prepare for CBSE Physical Education, choose PE-related courses, and understand school sports work. It is study support. It is not an official notification board.

Who writes the pages

Educational pages are written and reviewed by Jai Thakur (B.P.Ed., M.P.Ed., NIS Taekwondo Coach; Physical Education Teacher and tournament organiser based in Alwar). Guest content is not currently published.

What we will not invent

  • University fees, rankings, placement statistics or “guaranteed jobs”
  • NCTE or UGC recognition for a specific programme unless we point you to the official check
  • Admission closing dates copied from memory
  • Copied textbook paragraphs

University and recruitment pages

These pages describe location, commonly offered PE programme types, typical eligibility patterns, document lists and questions you should ask the admission office. Fees appear only as “verify on the official prospectus”. Recognition is stated as something you must confirm on the NCTE and university websites for the current session.

CBSE notes

Unit pages explain concepts in original language, with examples, exam-style questions and common mistakes. They are aligned to the public CBSE Physical Education syllabus structure (unit titles). They are not a substitute for the prescribed textbook or the current CBSE circular.

Corrections

Send the URL and a source to info@physicaleducationcareer.in. Factual errors are corrected as soon as they are confirmed. We do not silently rewrite history to hide a previous error; we update the page and, where needed, add a short note.

Advertising

Advertisements, if shown, are served by third parties. Advertisers do not choose university recommendations or exam answers. See Privacy Policy.
