CBSE school games: a PE teacher’s working checklist

CBSE sports (often called CBSE Games) are run from circulars issued to affiliated schools, not from blogs. This page does not publish registration dates, a sports list for a given year, or “registration open” status. Those lines live in the circular.

A file named cbse-games-2026.pdf is stored on this site as a copy of a circular. Open it and read the year, sports and dates printed inside. If CBSE has issued a newer circular, use the newer one from the Board — not this filename.

What the circular is for

It tells principals and PE teachers how students from CBSE-affiliated schools enter cluster / zonal / national level competitions: eligibility, age groups, documents, online portal steps (if any), and which disciplines are offered that year.

School-side process (typical — confirm in the PDF)

  1. Principal nominates a PE teacher as the sports in-charge.
  2. Students’ names, class, date of birth and sport are checked against school records (not against a coaching academy certificate alone).
  3. Documents commonly demanded in past years: school ID, birth proof as specified, photographs, parental consent, medical fitness if asked. Copy the list from this year’s circular.
  4. Entries go through the school, not through a student’s personal WhatsApp to CBSE.

Age categories and sports list

Do not memorise last year’s under-14 / under-17 cut-off dates. Age is calculated as the circular states. The list of sports changes; taekwondo, athletics, basketball and others may or may not appear in a given circular.

Documents folder for the PE office

  • Printed circular + highlighting of deadlines
  • Student list signed by class teacher / principal
  • ID and DOB proofs in the format named
  • Travel and escort plan if the cluster venue is away

How this connects to PE teaching

Selecting a school team is not the same as intramurals. Intramurals belong in Class 12 Unit 1 and the school tournament checklist. CBSE games are an extramural, affiliation-based championship.
