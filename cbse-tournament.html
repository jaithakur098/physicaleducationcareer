About CBSE Games 2026

CBSE Games 2026 is one of the largest school sports events conducted under the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE). Thousands of students from CBSE affiliated schools across India participate in various sports disciplines every year. The tournament provides a platform for young athletes to showcase their talent, improve their skills, and compete at different levels of competition.

The CBSE Sports Programme aims to promote physical fitness, discipline, sportsmanship, teamwork and leadership among school students. Through these competitions, students get an opportunity to represent their schools and gain valuable experience in competitive sports.

Objectives of CBSE Sports

The main objective of CBSE Sports is to encourage participation in games and physical activities. Sports play a vital role in the overall development of students by improving physical health, mental well-being, confidence, decision-making ability and social interaction.

CBSE believes that sports and education should go hand in hand. Therefore, schools are encouraged to provide equal opportunities for students to participate in different sporting events throughout the academic year.

Important Information for Participants

Students participating in CBSE Games should ensure that all required documents are completed before registration. Schools must verify student details, age eligibility and participation requirements as per the latest CBSE Sports Guidelines.

  • School ID Card
  • Aadhar Card
  • Birth Certificate
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • School Verification Documents
  • Sports Entry Forms

Sports Disciplines

CBSE Games include a wide range of sports disciplines such as Athletics, Taekwondo, Basketball, Football, Volleyball, Kho-Kho, Kabaddi, Swimming, Chess, Badminton, Table Tennis, Yoga and many other events. The list may vary according to CBSE Sports notifications issued for a particular year.

Registration Process

Schools can register eligible students through the official CBSE Sports portal. Registration dates, eligibility criteria, age categories and participation guidelines are announced through official CBSE circulars and notifications.

Participants are advised to regularly check official updates regarding registration schedules, venue details, tournament dates and event-specific instructions.

Benefits of Participation

Participation in CBSE Games helps students develop confidence, discipline, teamwork and leadership skills. Sports competitions also provide opportunities for students to gain recognition at district, state and national levels.

Outstanding performers may receive opportunities for higher-level competitions and sports scholarships in future.
