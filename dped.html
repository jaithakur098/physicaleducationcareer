D.P.Ed Admission 2026

What is D.P.Ed?

D.P.Ed (Diploma in Physical Education) ek 1 year diploma course hai jo Physical Education Teacher aur Sports Coach banne ke liye kiya jata hai.

Eligibility

  • 10+2 Pass
  • Minimum 50% Marks
  • Sports Interest Preferred

Course Duration

1 Year Diploma Program

Career Opportunities

  • PET Teacher
  • Sports Coach
  • Fitness Trainer
  • Sports Academy Instructor

Application Form → Document Verification → Admission Confirmation
