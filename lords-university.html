About Lords University

Lords University is a multidisciplinary private university located in Alwar, Rajasthan. The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate and research programmes in Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Agriculture, Allied Health Sciences, Education and several professional disciplines. The university focuses on outcome-based learning, practical training and industry-oriented education to prepare students for successful careers.

For students interested in Physical Education, Lords University provides an academic environment that encourages physical fitness, sports participation, leadership development and professional growth. The university aims to combine classroom learning with practical sports activities so that students develop both theoretical knowledge and practical teaching skills required in the field of Physical Education.

The university campus provides modern infrastructure, experienced faculty members, digital learning facilities and student support services. Students also get opportunities to participate in sports competitions, cultural programmes, seminars, workshops and personality development activities throughout the academic session.
