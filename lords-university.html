Lords University, Alwar — Physical Education checklist

Lords University is a private university in Alwar. For students already living in Alwar (including those training at local academies), the practical advantage is shorter travel for counselling and PFT — not a ranking, and not a substitute for NCTE checks.

Confirm programmes and fees on lordsuniversity.edu.in. This page does not state that any PE programme is currently NCTE-recognised.

Why Alwar is a different decision from Jaipur

Jaipur options on this site include University of Rajasthan and NIMS. Alwar means a different city, different hostel market, and a different application calendar. If you work or teach in Alwar, ask whether theory classes clash with school duty hours — that is a local logistics question, not a syllabus question.

What to verify on campus

  • Exact PE programme names for this session (B.P.Ed vs B.P.E.S vs diploma).
  • Where practicals are held: own ground vs hired municipal ground.
  • PFT events and whether they are qualifying or scored.

Documents (typical)

  • 10th/12th and graduation (for B.P.Ed)
  • B.P.Ed degree for M.P.Ed if offered
  • Category / sports certificates only if the notice gives weightage

Course process: B.P.Ed · admissions overview. Contact for counselling (not an official university office): contact.
