NIMS University, Jaipur — Physical Education checklist

NIMS is a private university in Jaipur. Unlike a single-faculty PE college, the campus is known for a wide mix of professional programmes (including medical and other faculties). That matters for PE students because you should check whether PE practicals share grounds with other departments and how far hostels sit from the track and courts.

This page does not publish fees, placements or NCTE status. Confirm programme names, intake and recognition on nimsuniversity.org and on the NCTE public listing for the same programme title you will join.

Why this campus is different from Uniraj

University of Rajasthan is a state university in Jaipur with a long-standing PE department. NIMS is a private campus. Compare: application portal, fee letterhead, hostel rules, and whether admission uses a university entrance plus PFT. Do not assume the same dates or the same NCTE file as Uniraj.

Questions that only make sense at NIMS

  • Where is the PE department relative to the medical/engineering blocks — can you reach the ground between theory classes?
  • Is the PFT conducted on campus, and is a list of events published?
  • Who issues the fee receipt (university accounts vs a third party)?

Admission process (generic private-university pattern)

  1. Apply on the official admission form for this session.
  2. Appear for written test and/or PFT if notified.
  3. Document verification: 10th/12th, graduation, B.P.Ed for M.P.Ed, category papers if claimed.
  4. Pay only after a written fee structure on university letterhead.
