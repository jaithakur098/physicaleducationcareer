About NIMS University Jaipur

NIMS University Jaipur is one of Rajasthan's leading private universities offering professional education in Medical, Engineering, Management, Law, Agriculture and Physical Education. The university is recognised by UGC and offers quality infrastructure, experienced faculty, sports facilities and modern laboratories.

Students from different states of India take admission every year in Physical Education programmes because of practical training, sports facilities and career opportunities.

What to inspect on a Jaipur campus visit

NIMS is a large private campus on the Jaipur–Delhi side of the city. For Physical Education, a visit should answer practical questions, not a brochure list of “international stadiums”.

  • Where PE theory classes are held, and where practicals (track, courts, gym) actually take place.
  • Whether hostel rooms for PE students are on the same campus as the grounds you will use at 6 a.m.
  • Who the PE faculty are, and whether the B.P.Ed / M.P.Ed intake for this session is open.
  • How the university conducts the physical fitness test, if any — surface, events, and qualifying marks.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is NIMS University recognised?

NIMS University is a private university established under the Rajasthan State Act. Students should always verify the latest university and course approvals before taking admission.

Does NIMS University offer B.P.Ed.?

Students should check the latest admission notification for the current session to confirm programme availability and eligibility.

Can students from other states apply?

Yes. Eligible students from different states can apply according to the university admission rules.

Is hostel facility available?

Separate hostel facilities are available for boys and girls. Students should confirm availability during admission.
