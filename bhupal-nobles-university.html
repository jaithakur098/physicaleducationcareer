Bhupal Nobles’ University, Udaipur

Bhupal Nobles’ University is a private university in Udaipur (southern Rajasthan). Students from Mewar, south Rajasthan and nearby Gujarat often shortlist it to avoid relocating to Jaipur. That is a geography choice — still run NCTE and fee checks.

Official site listed previously: bnuniversity.ac.in. Confirm PE programme names and recognition there. This page does not publish placements or fee amounts.

What to inspect in Udaipur that you cannot see online

  • Distance from hostel/PG areas to the PE ground in peak traffic.
  • Whether athletics practicals use the university ground or a hired municipal stadium.
  • Monsoon drainage on outdoor courts (Udaipur rainfall is not the same as desert campuses).

Courses

Ask the admission office which of B.P.Ed / M.P.Ed / B.P.E.S are open this session. Process outline: B.P.Ed · M.P.Ed.

Do not confuse with other Udaipur institutions

Udaipur has multiple colleges and universities. Use the legal name Bhupal Nobles’ University on receipts and NCTE searches so you do not check the wrong institution’s recognition file.
