Training and Doping in Sports

Sports training is a planned process that improves physical fitness and sports performance.

Principles of Training

  • Overload
  • Progression
  • Specificity
  • Recovery

Doping

Doping is the use of prohibited substances or methods to improve sports performance unfairly.

Effects of Doping

  • Health risks
  • Disqualification
  • Loss of reputation
  • Violation of sports ethics
