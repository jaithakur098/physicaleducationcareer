Unit 5: Sports and Nutrition

Nutrition plays a vital role in sports performance and overall health. A balanced diet provides the energy and nutrients required for growth, development, recovery and optimum athletic performance. Proper nutrition helps athletes maintain fitness, improve performance and prevent injuries.

Meaning of Nutrition

Nutrition is the science of food and its relationship with health. It involves the intake of nutrients that are necessary for the proper functioning of the body.

Importance of Nutrition in Sports

  • Provides energy for physical activities.
  • Supports growth and development.
  • Helps in muscle repair and recovery.
  • Maintains healthy body weight.
  • Improves athletic performance.
  • Prevents nutritional deficiencies.

Balanced Diet

A balanced diet is a diet that contains all essential nutrients in appropriate amounts to meet the body's requirements.

Components of Balanced Diet

  • Carbohydrates
  • Proteins
  • Fats
  • Vitamins
  • Minerals
  • Water
  • Roughage

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are the primary source of energy for the body. They are especially important for athletes participating in high-intensity activities.

Functions of Carbohydrates

  • Provide energy.
  • Maintain blood glucose levels.
  • Support muscle activity.
  • Prevent fatigue.

Sources

  • Rice
  • Wheat
  • Bread
  • Potatoes
  • Fruits

Proteins

Proteins are body-building nutrients that help in growth, repair and maintenance of body tissues.

Functions of Proteins

  • Growth and development.
  • Repair of damaged tissues.
  • Formation of enzymes and hormones.
  • Muscle building.

Sources

  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Pulses
  • Fish
  • Meat

Fats

Fats are concentrated sources of energy and are essential for the absorption of certain vitamins.

Functions of Fats

  • Provide energy.
  • Protect internal organs.
  • Maintain body temperature.
  • Help absorb vitamins A, D, E and K.

Sources

  • Butter
  • Ghee
  • Nuts
  • Seeds
  • Vegetable oils

Vitamins

Vitamins are protective nutrients required in small amounts for normal body functions.

Importance of Vitamins

  • Support immunity.
  • Promote growth.
  • Maintain healthy skin and eyes.
  • Help metabolic activities.

Minerals

Minerals are inorganic substances required for proper growth and body functioning.

Important Minerals

  • Calcium
  • Iron
  • Phosphorus
  • Potassium
  • Sodium

Water

Water is essential for life and is often called the most important nutrient.

Functions of Water

  • Regulates body temperature.
  • Transports nutrients.
  • Removes waste products.
  • Maintains hydration.

Nutritive Components of Diet

  • Carbohydrates
  • Proteins
  • Fats
  • Vitamins
  • Minerals
  • Water

Non-Nutritive Components of Diet

  • Roughage
  • Dietary Fibre

Healthy Weight Management

Maintaining a healthy body weight is essential for sports performance and overall health. It can be achieved through balanced nutrition and regular physical activity.

Guidelines for Healthy Weight Management

  • Eat a balanced diet.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Stay hydrated.
  • Avoid junk food.
  • Get adequate sleep.

Eating for Weight Gain

  • Increase calorie intake.
  • Consume protein-rich foods.
  • Eat frequently.
  • Include healthy fats.

Eating for Weight Loss

  • Reduce calorie intake.
  • Increase physical activity.
  • Consume fruits and vegetables.
  • Avoid sugary foods and drinks.

Eating Disorders

Eating disorders are unhealthy eating habits that negatively affect physical and mental health.

Anorexia Nervosa

Anorexia Nervosa is a disorder characterized by extreme fear of gaining weight and severe restriction of food intake.

Bulimia Nervosa

Bulimia Nervosa is characterized by episodes of overeating followed by purging or excessive exercise.

Conclusion

Nutrition is essential for maintaining health and improving sports performance. A balanced diet provides energy, supports growth, promotes recovery and helps athletes achieve their full potential. Proper nutrition combined with regular exercise contributes to a healthy and active lifestyle.
