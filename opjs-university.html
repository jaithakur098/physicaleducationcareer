BPED, MPED & D.P.Ed Courses

The School of Physical Education at OPJS University offers professional programmes for students interested in teaching, coaching, fitness, sports science and sports management. These courses are designed according to university regulations and help students build a career in the Physical Education sector.

Bachelor of Physical Education (B.P.Ed.)

Bachelor of Physical Education (B.P.Ed.) is one of the most popular professional courses for students who wish to become Physical Education Teachers, Sports Coaches, Fitness Trainers, Sports Officers and Government PTIs.

  • Course Duration : 2 Years
  • Level : Undergraduate Professional Programme
  • Mode : Regular
  • Admission : Merit / University Rules
  • Internship & Practical Training Included

Master of Physical Education (M.P.Ed.)

Master of Physical Education is suitable for students who have already completed B.P.Ed. and wish to become Assistant Professors, Sports Scientists, Researchers or Senior Physical Education Teachers.

  • Course Duration : 2 Years
  • Research Opportunities
  • Sports Psychology
  • Sports Biomechanics
  • Exercise Physiology
  • Advanced Coaching

Diploma in Physical Education (D.P.Ed.)

D.P.Ed. is a diploma programme designed for students interested in school physical education, sports coaching and fitness instruction.
