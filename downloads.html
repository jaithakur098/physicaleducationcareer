What you can download from this page

Everything in this section is free and requires no registration. The files are grouped so that a student preparing for the CBSE board exam, and a graduate preparing for a B.P.Ed entrance or a PTI recruitment test, can each find the right material without hunting through the whole site.

  • Previous year question papers — CBSE Physical Education papers for board revision, with our answer guidance.
  • Unit-wise notes — condensed notes for Class 11 and Class 12, matched to the current syllabus.
  • CBSE circulars — official information relating to CBSE games and tournaments.
  • Syllabus and course material — reference material relevant to B.P.Ed and M.P.Ed programmes.

How to use the PDFs effectively

A downloaded PDF only helps if it becomes handwriting. Print or open one paper, attempt it against a clock without notes, and mark it the same day. Keep one notebook for definitions you could not reproduce and one for diagrams you could not draw cleanly; revise only those two notebooks in the last week before your exam. Students who use the papers this way consistently outperform students who accumulate files they never attempt.
