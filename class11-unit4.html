Physical Education and Sports for CWSN

Children With Special Needs (CWSN) require equal opportunities in physical education and sports. Inclusive education helps them participate actively in school activities and develop confidence.

Importance of Inclusive Education

  • Promotes social integration
  • Improves self-confidence
  • Develops motor skills
  • Provides equal opportunities
  • Enhances physical fitness

Benefits of Sports for CWSN

  • Better physical health
  • Improved emotional well-being
  • Greater independence
  • Development of teamwork skills
