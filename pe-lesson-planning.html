PE teacher lesson planning

A PE period is not “free play until the bell”. Inspectors and principals look for an objective, organisation, and a way to know whether students improved. The template below fits a 40-minute secondary period on one court.

Write the objective in one line

Bad: “Students will play basketball.” Better: “Students will perform a two-handed chest pass to a moving partner over 6 metres with the ball arriving at chest height.”

Sample 40-minute plan (chest pass)

MinutesActivityOrganisation
0–5Roll call, safety (jewellery, footwear), pulse-raising jog, dynamic armsOne grid
5–12Skill: stationary chest pass, 8–10 m apartPairs, extra balls at the side
12–22Skill under pressure: pass after one bounce; then to a moving partnerSame pairs, then rotate
22–35Small-sided game: 3v3, score only from a chest pass assistTwo half-courts if numbers allow
35–40Cool-down, two questions: “What did your hands do on the catch?”Circle

Inclusion

Give a sitting passing option, a closer distance, or a larger ball. CWSN planning ideas: Class 12 Unit 4 and Class 11 Unit 4.

Assessment without a clipboard army

Pick four students to watch for the objective. Note yes/no. Next lesson, watch four different students. That is enough for a term grade conversation.
