PET Career Roadmap

Become a Government PE Teacher

Step-by-step guidance to become a Physical Education Teacher (PET / PTI / PGT PE) in India.

Career Path

4-Step Career Roadmap

1. Qualification

Complete D.P.Ed., B.P.Ed. or M.P.Ed. from an NCTE-approved institution.

2. Competitive Exams

Prepare for CTET, State TET, DSSSB, KVS, NVS, RPSC PTI and other exams.

3. Interview & Demo

Build subject knowledge, confidence and teaching skills.

4. Selection

Get selected as PET, PTI, TGT PE or PGT PE in schools.
