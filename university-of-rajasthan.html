About the University

The University of Rajasthan, Jaipur, is one of the oldest state universities in Rajasthan. Its Department of Physical Education has been functioning since the mid-20th century and offers specialised programmes in Physical Education, Yoga and research. The department introduced B.P.Ed. in 1994–95 and M.P.Ed. in 1995–96, and also offers research opportunities and yoga programmes. Department history (official page — verify)

The university conducts Physical Education admissions according to its current admission policy. Historically, B.P.Ed. and M.P.Ed. admissions have involved a centralised process with physical fitness requirements. Applicants should always check the latest admission notification for the current session. Official department site

Department of Physical Education

The Department of Physical Education at the University of Rajasthan is one of the oldest Physical Education departments in Rajasthan. It offers professional programmes in Physical Education and Yoga with an emphasis on sports science, physical fitness, coaching, teaching and research. Physical Fitness Test

For the Physical Education programmes, the department has historically prescribed a compulsory Physical Fitness Test. The published criteria have included running events such as:

  • B.P.Ed. Boys – 2200 metres
  • B.P.Ed. Girls – 1800 metres
  • M.P.Ed. Boys – 2400 metres
  • M.P.Ed. Girls – 2000 metres

Applicants should always verify the latest admission notification because requirements may be revised for a new admission session. ([Department of Physical Education](https://physicaleducation.uniraj.ac.in/history.php))
